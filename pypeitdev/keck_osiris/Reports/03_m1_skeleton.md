## 2. Frame typing and the static files

Frame-typing rules implemented (`check_frame_type`):

| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| `dark` | `dispname == 'Drk'` |
| `standard` | on-sky (`idname` in a/s, not dark) and (`idname == 's'` **or** target name carries a spectral-type tag such as `(A0V)` **or** a bright-star catalogue name - HD, HIP, HR, BD, SAO, TYC, FS, GD, HZ, Feige, LTT, EG - with exptime <= 60 s) |
| `science`, `trace`, `arc`, `tilt` | on-sky and not standard |
| `sky` | on-sky and `ISSKY == 1` (science or standard) |
| everything else | never |

Epoch selection for the wavelength coefficients picks the latest epoch
whose start is on or before `MJD-OBS`.  (The DRP's `where(mjd ge begin
and mjd le end)` with end dates at 00:00 leaves a one-day hole at every
epoch boundary; the table stores the inclusive end and the selection
avoids the hole.)

## 5. Deviations from the design document

1. **`specflip = True`** (design: False).  The Keck coefficients give
   pixel decreasing with wavelength (lenslet (33,25): 1680 px at 2000 nm,
   615 px at 2300 nm), and PypeIt requires wavelength to increase with
   pixel.  Consequence for M2/M3: the rectification matrix is in the raw
   orientation, so a `RawImage.rectify` step placed after `orient` must
   transpose *and* flip the matrix (or un-orient the image).
2. **Standards are recognised by target name**, not only by
   `OBSTYPE == 's'`: every KOA frame (including the A0V stars and the
   darks) has `OBSTYPE = 'a'`.  Science and standard are made mutually
   exclusive.  See Q&A 1.
3. **`exptime` units decided by the era** (`MJD-OBS >= 57388` -> ms),
   with `ITIME > 2000` only as a fallback when `MJD-OBS` is missing.  The
   plain `ITIME > 2000` rule mis-reads 2-s exposures (`ITIME = 2000` ms)
   as 2000 s; the KOA set has twelve such frames (7 HD89239, 5 darks).
4. **`instrument`: `INSTRUME` first, `CURRINST` fallback** (design:
   the reverse).  The 2024 darks have `CURRINST = 'LRISADC'` (the
   instrument selected at the telescope during the afternoon), while
   `INSTRUME = 'OSIRIS'`.
5. **New metadata keys in `pypeit/core/meta.py`** (`issky`, `sampmode`,
   `numreads`, `tmatemp`): PypeIt validates every spectrograph meta key
   against the global metadata model, so the design's keys had to be
   added there (shared code, 8 lines).
6. **Sky frames in the dev-suite pypeit files are `sky` (not
   `science`)**, so they are subtracted but not reduced on their own;
   `pypeit_setup` still types them `science,sky` automatically (design
   4.3).  See Q&A 3.
7. `config_specific_par` sets the boxcar radius (design 4.8 lists it as a
   default; it depends on `SSCALE`, hence configuration-specific).
8. `TRUITIME` **is** present in the 2024 headers (Report 01 found it
   absent in 2015/2016) and differs from `ITIME` by up to 36 % for short
   frames (1.475 s vs 2 s).  *Updated after Q&A 2 was answered:* the
   exposure time is now `TRUITIME x COADDS` whenever `TRUITIME` is
   present, falling back to the `ITIME` rules of item 3.  Both the DN
   conversion and the `exptime` metadata use this value, so the
   `exptime` column of `keck_osiris_kbb_050.pypeit` was regenerated
   from the headers (e.g. 600.0 -> 598.963, 2.0 -> 1.475); the frame
   typing of Section 4.1 is unchanged.

Items 1, 2 and 6 were confirmed by the M1 Q&A answers (4, 1 and 3)
and are now recorded in the design document (decision table rows
M1-1 to M1-4, Sections 4.2-4.4).

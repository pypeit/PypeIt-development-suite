# Keck/OSIRIS milestone M4: science-mode reduction of NGC 3245 (Kbb/050)

## Goals

Reduce the KOA 2024-02-03 Kbb/050 set end to end: A-B and OH-scaled sky subtraction,
OH wavelength refinement, datacube through a generic fiber-cube script, and telluric
correction with the A0V standards.  Requires the Kbb/050 rectification matrix from Keck
in `RAW_DATA/keck_osiris/Kbb_050/calib/`.

## Tasks

1. Confirm the matrix is present; if not, stop and say so in Q&A.
2. A-B / A-sky through `bkg_id` with no dark subtraction (Q&A 23);
   darks only for unpaired frames, grouped by ITIME.
3. Davies (2007) OH-scaled sky subtraction: `osiris_oh_scaling` option
   in `SkySubPar` (`add-parameter`); implementation in
   `pypeit/core/skysub.py` (group OH lines, fit per-group scale factors
   between science and sky spectra per lenslet or per block, apply
   before subtraction); call it from `FiberFindObjects` when set.
4. OH wavelength refinement: an OSIRIS OH line list in
   `pypeit/data/arc_lines/lists/`, `reference = 'sky'`, per-lenslet
   zero-point correction with a QA plot of the shift statistics;
   keep the Keck solution where fewer than 3 lines are usable.
5. Generic `pypeit_fiber_cube` script (`new-script`) on
   `core/datacube.build_cube_from_spec1d` and the spectrograph's fiber
   hooks; make `pypeit_binospec_ifu_cube` a thin alias (Q&A 24).  Build
   the NGC 3245 cube with the lenslet grid, SSCALE and position-angle
   WCS.
6. Telluric correction and flux calibration with HD101060 using
   PypeIt's IR sensfunc/telluric tools; check the K-band telluric grid.
7. Dev suite: `keck_osiris/Kbb_050` reduce test, telluric test, vet
   tests (cube structure, OH-line tilt test on an ISSKY=1 frame).

## Acceptance

NGC 3245 reduced end to end: emission-line map and continuum image
look physical; sky residuals at OH lines are reduced by the scaling
step relative to plain A-B (quantify); telluric-corrected A0V spectrum
flat to a few percent; dev-suite reduce, afterburn and vet tests for
`Kbb_050` pass.

Write `pypeitdev/keck_osiris/Reports/06_m4_science_mode.md` describing what was
built, how it was verified (commands and outputs), and anything that
deviates from the design document, and update
`keck_osiris_implementation.md` if the plan changed.
